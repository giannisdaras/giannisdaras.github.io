I work on **generative AI**. I am particularly interested in **data-efficient learning**.

My group's broad topics of interest are: learning from imperfect data, diffusion models, data-centric AI, inverse problems, AI4Science, and agents. See the [research lab](/lab/) page for the people.

Before that I was a postdoctoral associate at [MIT CSAIL](https://www.csail.mit.edu/), working with [Costis Daskalakis](https://people.csail.mit.edu/costis/) and [Antonio Torralba](https://groups.csail.mit.edu/vision/torralbalab/). I did my Ph.D. in Computer Science at UT Austin with [Alexandros Dimakis](https://users.ece.utexas.edu/~dimakis/), and my undergraduate degree in ECE at the National Technical University of Athens. I have interned at NVIDIA Research and Google Research.

**I am always looking for strong students.** If you are interested in working with me, send me an email with your CV and what you would like to work on.
