I am an Assistant Professor at MIT. I work on **generative AI**. I am particularly interested in **data-efficient learning**.

My group's broad topics of interest are: learning from imperfect data, diffusion models, data-centric AI, inverse problems, AI4Science, and agents.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral associate at [MIT CSAIL](https://www.csail.mit.edu/), working with [Costis Daskalakis](https://people.csail.mit.edu/costis/) and [Antonio Torralba](https://groups.csail.mit.edu/vision/torralbalab/). I did my Ph.D. in Computer Science at UT Austin with [Alexandros Dimakis](https://users.ece.utexas.edu/~dimakis/), and my undergraduate degree in ECE at the National Technical University of Athens. I have interned at NVIDIA Research (2023, with [Arash Vahdat](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=p9-nlRIAAAAJ&hl=en)) and Google Research (2022, with [Peyman Milanfar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=iGzDl8IAAAAJ&hl=en), [Mauricio Delbracio](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=lDDm920AAAAJ&hl=en), Hossein Talebi, [Vincent Chu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=R-OrlSgAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Abhishek Kumar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=6vghMS0AAAAJ&hl=en)).

**I am always looking for strong students.** If you are interested in working with me, send me an email with your CV and what you would like to work on.
